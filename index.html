How we think about it

Not every problem has a Workday answer.

Configuration is easy to add and expensive to remove. So the first question is whether the problem belongs in Workday, beside it, or nowhere, and we would rather spend time there than on a build that has to be unwound in two years.

The firm

Founded 2014

Van Field Group is small on purpose. Engagements are staffed from a standing bench of specialists rather than assembled from whoever is available, and the people who scope the work are the people who do it.

Most engagements run long. Many span a full implementation cycle and continue into post-production support, which means the team that made the design decisions is still there when those decisions get tested.

02

Financial services

Broadcasters, nonprofits, and institutions running lean back offices against complex realities. Grant and program-funded cost allocation, mixed populations of staff, freelance, and union talent, and reporting written for a board rather than a CFO.

High-volume hourly workforces at scale. Time tracking, absence, and benefits eligibility across thousands of variable-hour employees, plus the carrier connectivity that has to be right before open enrollment opens.
